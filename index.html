About this tool

The Warehouse Emissions Estimator is designed to allow users to see the baseline emissions for 1,000 warehouses in Illinois, as well as an estimated WPRA scenario and customizable outputs. This represents ~1/7 of the total statewide warehouse population of 50,000+ sq ft.

This tool was created to support the Clear the Air Coalition in their efforts to implement the proposed Warehouse Pollution Reduction Act.
